**Effective Date:** 01 February 2026

This privacy policy applies to the **Flip 'EM!** app ("Application") created by **Michael Angerer, Germany** ("Service Provider") as an ad-supported service.


## Overview

The Application is provided as an ad-supported mobile game. The Service Provider does not require user registration and does not directly collect or store personal data. However, third-party advertising services integrated into the Application may automatically collect certain information.


## Information Collection and Use

The Service Provider does not directly collect personally identifiable information. Third-party services used in the Application may collect information automatically to provide and improve their services.

This information may include:

- Device IP address
- Advertising identifiers and device identifiers
- Operating system and version
- App usage data and session duration
- Approximate location (country or region)

This data is used for:

- Ad delivery and optimization
- Ad personalization (where consent is given)
- Fraud prevention and security
- Analytics and performance measurement

Processing is based on user consent where required (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) or legitimate interest in operating an ad-supported service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).


## Third-Party Services

The Application uses third-party services that may collect and process data used to identify you.

Third-party privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob / Google Privacy Policy** — [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

These partners may use cookies, advertising IDs, and similar technologies to provide personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure ad performance.

Data may be shared with advertising and measurement partners as part of ad delivery.


## Advertising & Consent

Where required by law (such as in the EU/EEA, UK, or California), the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request user consent for personalized advertising.

Users may choose between personalized and non-personalized ads where available and can change their consent preferences at any time through the in-app **Privacy Settings** menu.


## iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS devices, the Application may request permission to track users across apps and websites using Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Users can allow or deny this permission at any time in iOS system settings.


## Geolocation

The Application does not collect precise real-time location data. Advertising partners may derive coarse location information from IP address for regional ad targeting.


## Data Retention

The Service Provider does not retain personal data. Third-party partners retain data according to their own retention schedules and privacy policies.


## International Data Transfers

Third-party partners may process data on servers located outside your country of residence, including the United States and other countries. Appropriate safeguards are applied by those providers according to their policies.


## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access your personal data
- Request deletion of your data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Withdraw consent
- Object to or restrict processing
- Opt out of sale or sharing of data (where applicable)

Requests regarding data collected by advertising partners should be directed to those partners directly.


## Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children.


## Security

Reasonable safeguards are used through trusted third-party providers. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.


## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Users will be notified of material changes via the app or the app store listing. The updated policy will always show a revised effective date.
